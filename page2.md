## Probability Density Function of a Normal Distribution
The Probability Density Function (PDF) of a Normal Distribution follows the form
\\[ 
f_X(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}}\cdot e ^ {-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}.
\\]
